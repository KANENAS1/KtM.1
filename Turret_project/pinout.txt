Pan STEP: GPIO 17 (Physical Pin 11)
Pan DIR: GPIO 27 (Pin 13)
Pan EN: GPIO 22 (Pin 15) - LOW to enable
Tilt STEP: GPIO 23 (Pin 16)
Tilt DIR: GPIO 24 (Pin 18)
Tilt EN: GPIO 25 (Pin 22) - LOW to enable
Laser TTL: GPIO 18 (Pin 12) - HIGH to fire

Common GND for all. External 12V for steppers/drivers. Add 1kΩ series on STEP/DIR, 10kΩ pull-down on EN to GND.
Decoupling caps: 100µF + 0.1µF on driver VMOT/GND.
